Why It Works

A logo on a banner gets ignored.
A brand at mile two doesn't.

Run clubs are one of the few places a brand can show up in person, on a schedule, with an audience that already trusts the host. Here's what that's worth.

01

A warm, repeat audience

Same people, same night, every week. Trust and brand affinity compound with repetition in a way a one-off ad never gets the chance to.

02

High-intent, not passive

People who show up to run are already spending on gear, hydration, and recovery. Sampling to an audience like this converts at a real rate — brands report roughly 1 in 4 or 5 sampled runners becoming buyers.

03

Content, every single week

Every run is a photo and video opportunity. Your activation doesn't disappear after one night — it becomes ongoing content for both of us.
