About Us

Not your average run club

Started with a few friends, a speaker, and way too much energy. Now it's Houston's flyest weekly run — DJs, good lighting, better company.

We run first, vibe after. Bring a friend, bring five, or roll up solo — every pace is the right pace, and nobody's getting left behind.
