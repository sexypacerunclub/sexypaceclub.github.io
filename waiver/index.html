Waiver, Release of Liability & Assumption of Risk

Sexy Pace Running Club — Houston, TX

By checking the waiver box on our check-in form, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. Assumption of Risk

Participating in Sexy Pace Running Club activities — including group runs, walks, and related social events — involves inherent risks, including but not limited to: traffic and vehicles, uneven or wet surfaces, weather conditions, collision with other participants or third parties, and physical exertion including risk of cardiac events, muscle strain, dehydration, or other injury. You voluntarily assume all such risks, known and unknown.

2. Release of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by Texas law, you release, waive, and discharge Sexy Pace Running Club, its organizers, volunteers, and event partners/sponsors from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action arising out of or related to any loss, damage, or injury, including death, that may be sustained while participating in Sexy Pace Running Club activities — except for injury caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Sexy Pace Running Club or its organizers.

3. Medical Fitness

You confirm that you are physically fit to participate in group running activities, or have consulted a physician who has approved your participation. You are solely responsible for knowing your own physical limits.

4. Emergency Contact

You agree to provide a current emergency contact who can be reached in case of injury or medical emergency during an event.

5. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any dispute arising from this agreement will be resolved in Harris County, Texas.

6. Participants Under 18

Participants under the age of 18 must have this waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian.

Photo & Video

Separately from this waiver, our check-in form asks whether you consent to being photographed or filmed at events for social media and promotional use. This is optional and does not affect your ability to participate.
