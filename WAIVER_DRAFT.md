# Sexy Pace Running Club — Participant Waiver (DRAFT)

> ⚠️ **NOT LEGAL ADVICE. This is a starting draft, not a finished legal document.**
> Have an actual Texas-licensed attorney review this before relying on it. Texas
> requires waiver language releasing a party from its own negligence to be clear,
> unambiguous, and conspicuous within the document (the "express negligence
> doctrine") — a poorly worded waiver can be unenforceable even if everyone signs
> it. This draft follows the general shape of a standard participant waiver but
> has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

**Status:** Draft, not yet in use. Track changes to this file over time via git —
that gives you a dated record of what waiver language was in effect when, which
matters if it's ever challenged.

## Sexy Pace Running Club
### Waiver, Release of Liability, and Assumption of Risk

By checking the box below and typing my full legal name, I acknowledge and agree
to the following:

**1. Assumption of Risk.** I understand that participating in Sexy Pace Running
Club activities — including but not limited to group runs, walks, and related
social events — involves inherent risks, including but not limited to: traffic
and vehicles, uneven or wet surfaces, weather conditions, collision with other
participants or third parties, and physical exertion including risk of cardiac
events, muscle strain, dehydration, or other injury. I voluntarily assume all
such risks, known and unknown.

**2. Release of Liability.** To the fullest extent permitted by Texas law, I
release, waive, and discharge Sexy Pace Running Club, its organizers, volunteers,
and event partners/sponsors from any and all liability, claims, demands, or
causes of action arising out of or related to any loss, damage, or injury,
including death, that may be sustained by me while participating in Sexy Pace
Running Club activities, EXCEPT for injury caused by the gross negligence or
willful misconduct of Sexy Pace Running Club or its organizers.

**3. Medical Fitness.** I confirm that I am physically fit to participate in
group running activities, or have consulted a physician who has approved my
participation. I am solely responsible for knowing my own physical limits.

**4. Emergency Contact.** I have provided a current emergency contact who can be
reached in case of injury or medical emergency during an event.

**5. Governing Law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Texas. Any dispute arising from this agreement will be resolved in Harris
County, Texas.

**6. Participants Under 18.** Participants under the age of 18 must have this
waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. [CONFIRM: does the club actually
want to allow minors? If not, state a minimum age instead of a minor's-consent
clause — simpler and removes an entire category of extra liability

## Open questions for you / Maria / an attorney to resolve before this goes live

1. **Minors policy** — does the club actually have participants under 18? If
   never, say so explicitly and drop the parental-consent clause entirely
   (simpler, and removes a whole category of liability exposure question).
2. **"Gross negligence" carve-out** — this is standard and often required for
   enforceability, but confirm with a lawyer whether Texas caselaw wants
   different specific phrasing for a running club specifically.
3. **Sponsor/partner names** — should specific named sponsors (SISE Hydration,
   Las Perras Cafe, etc. for one-off events) also be named as released parties
   for events done in collaboration with them? Worth asking each partner
   whether they want to be named, or handle it with a separate event-specific
   addendum for collab events.
